Staying safe

A few simple habits keep your XRP safe.

Never share your seed or secret key

Your seed (secret key or recovery phrase) controls your wallet. Anyone who has it can take your funds. No real tool or support person needs it.

Ripple and Xaman will never DM you

Messages offering help, giveaways or "wallet verification" are scams. Ignore them.

Check the URL

Scam sites copy real ones. Look carefully at the address bar before you connect or type anything.

This site never asks for secrets

The tools here only ever need a public address (starting with "r"). If a page claiming to be this site asks for a seed, close it.

Verify it yourself

Everything is open source, so you can read the code and run it yourself:
