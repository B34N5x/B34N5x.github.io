Interactive preview

Follow a payment on the ledger

This walkthrough shows how a transaction moves, then labels a real Testnet payment in plain words. When you finish, you can choose to try the same process with your own wallet.

  • A payment that has already validated on the ledger cannot be reversed. Cancel has to happen before you sign.
  • You can press Cancel at every step before signing. Rejecting a request in Xaman also cancels it.
  • The demo uses throwaway Testnet wallets created in this browser and funded by the public faucet. This page never asks for your seed or secret, and it never signs with your wallet.
  • For something you can cancel later, you can draft an Escrow with a cancel time. After that time you can send an EscrowCancel yourself.

General information only. Not financial advice. Testnet play funds are not real value.

Testnet practice

Send a demo payment on Testnet

This page will create two demo-only Testnet wallets in memory, ask the public faucet for play funds, and submit a fixed demo payment. Seeds are never shown and are forgotten afterwards. The amount is a fixed practice figure, not a suggestion.

Transaction type

An escrow is not an undo button for a payment that already settled. It is a separate transaction type with its own cancel path after the cancel time you choose.

After this time, you can send an EscrowCancel from your own wallet to return the locked funds to you. Until then the funds stay in escrow.
