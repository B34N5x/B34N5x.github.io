Walkthrough

Follow a payment

Watch a practice payment travel across the Testnet. Then you can draft one with your own wallet.

More detail
  • A payment that has already validated cannot be reversed. Cancel before you sign.
  • The demo uses throwaway Testnet wallets in this browser, funded by the public faucet. Seeds are never shown, and this page never signs with your wallet.
  • For something you can cancel later, you can draft an Escrow with a cancel time, then send an EscrowCancel yourself.
  • General information only. Not financial advice. Testnet play funds are not real value.
