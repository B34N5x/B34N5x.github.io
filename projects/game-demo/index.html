NFT Game World

Concept demo

NFT game demo: pick a sample NFT to equip

Tap a card, then hold the screen (or use the arrow keys and space) to play.

Concept demo only. The NFTs are randomly made-up examples, not real and not on the ledger.

How it works

Your NFTs become in-game gear. The game reads each NFT's traits and turns them into abilities: rarer weapons fire more shots, rarer armour blocks more hits, and a puppy companion barks and bites to protect you.

Later, mutable NFTs on the XRPL can save your progress, and other devs can plug in their own collections and events. Wallet sign-in will be read-only. Testnet first, always.

Demo uses made-up sample NFTs only.
