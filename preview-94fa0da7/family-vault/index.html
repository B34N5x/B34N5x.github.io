Family Vault

Savings for the young people in your family. It's theirs. You sign everything in your own wallet. This site never sees keys and stores nothing.

New here? The basics

  • Testnet is a practice XRP Ledger. Its test XRP is free and worth nothing, so you can learn without risk.
  • An escrow locks XRP on the ledger until a date you choose. Then it can be released to the person it's for.
  • A tx hash is the receipt ID for a transaction (64 characters). Save it somewhere private, then paste it here later to check progress.
  • Never share your seed or secret words. Not with us, not with anyone. Nobody legit will ever ask.
Your privacy
  • We keep nothing. No account, no cookies, no saved data. This page doesn't remember you.
  • Your tx hash is your bookmark. Save it yourself, then paste it here to pick up where you left off. The page reads it from the public XRPL ledger each time.
  • Closing the tab, or pressing "Clear this page", wipes everything you typed.
  • Escrows live on the public ledger. Anyone with the address can see them. Nobody, including us, can delete them. Only the owner can cancel or finish one from their own wallet, when its rules allow.
  • We never ask for your seed or keys. If anyone does, it's a scam.

Tip: keep your tx hashes somewhere private, like a note only you control.
